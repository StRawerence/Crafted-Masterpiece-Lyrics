[Vocalization]

[Intro]
A window glows where power’s gone…
A child counts what dawn has drawn…
Not gold—but clean and steady rain—
A future drawn without the pain…

[Verse]
他把舊輪胎做成月亮
教室在風裡微微搖晃
沒有課本，只有磚牆
寫滿那不肯熄滅的光

[Pre-Chorus]
From rising seas to dusty plains
We share one sky, one set of chains—
Not gifts—but rights we claim as due:
What’s yours is mine, and mine is you

[Chorus]
We walk as one beneath this sun
No voice lost when day is done
Not just words—but bridges built—
Where every crack with care is filled
Solidaridad, más que un grito…
Un mapa vivo, compartido
One breath One pulse One common ground—
Where silence breaks and hope is found

[Verse]
Él guarda su globo en un cajón
Sueña con cielos sin traición
Su isla se hunde, lento, sin voz—
Mas en sus ojos arde un sol

[Pre-Chorus]
視野遼闊，卻看不見遠方
思緒淹沒，追求著那場夢
牆面崩塌，才聽見心在喊話
推開風浪，我們能聽見對方

[Chorus]
一起走過這片燦爛陽光
沒有人該被獨自留下
用雙手築出未來的路
讓每一寸裂痕長出新芽
Sembramos juntos lo que el miedo rompió…
Raíces fuertes donde nada creció
同一片天、同一顆心、同一塊土地——
人心不再沈默，希望將會升起

[Bridge]
From eight old vows to seventeen ways
We stitch the world through better days
Not flawless—but still we choose…
To light what darkness covers through

[Chorus]
Caminamos juntos bajo este sol
Nadie se pierde al final
No son promesas—son actos reales—
Donde el valor cura los males
把光芒傳遞給另一雙眼睛
驅散恐懼 讓心在溫暖寧靜
Un mar Un cielo Un grito global—
Donde nadie camina en soledad

[Outro]
A lamp stays lit past midnight’s sigh…
For someone else’s fragile try
No grand stage—just open doors—
To share the world, and mend its floors
